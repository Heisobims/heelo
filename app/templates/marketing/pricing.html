How the fee works

Free events are free, always

A {{ platform_fee_percent }}% cut of free is free. There's no plan, no cap, no "upgrade to keep hosting free events."

Flexible fee strategy: Pass to buyer or absorb

You choose what fits your brand. Pass fees to the ticket buyer at checkout to net 100% of your ticket price, or offer inclusive pricing where fees are absorbed into ticket face value (ideal for galas, exclusive dinners, and conferences).

Refund a ticket, and we refund our cut too

If you refund an attendee, the fee on that ticket is reversed along with it. You never pay us for a sale that didn't end up happening.

You see the fee before you publish, not after

When you set a ticket price, you'll see exactly what you'll take home per ticket: no discovering it for the first time on your payout.
