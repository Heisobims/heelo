How payouts work
  • You receive 100% of the ticket price you set: Tixdoo's 5% fee and an estimated payment-processing fee are added on top at checkout and paid by the buyer, never deducted from you.
  • With Paystack, Flutterwave, or a verified Stripe account connected, that full amount settles automatically to your bank, typically within 24 hours after your event ends.
  • Free events have no platform fee.
  • Local accounts are reference-only records: they don't receive automatic payouts. Tixdoo tracks and settles what's owed to these accounts by hand; see your organization's payout history for details.
